Activities During Pchum Ben Day

During Pchum Ben, Cambodian people participate in many traditional activities.

Visit the Pagoda People go to the pagoda to make offerings and pray.
Offer Food to Monks Food, fruits, flowers, and other items are prepared to offer to the monks.
Pray for Ancestors People pray for the souls of their ancestors and wish them a better next life.
Spend Time with Family Families gather together and participate in the festival.
